# RunoffLab
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-proposed default: React, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js; pure numerical computation in a Web Worker. Local Python preparation. Static core, no account required.

## Users
Student environmental clubs and community groups exploring a small public site's response to hypothetical rain.

## Product Purpose
Apply an explicit storm to sourced terrain, compare a legal rain-garden intervention under the same conditions, inspect physical accounting, and reproduce the experiment.

## Positioning
Transparent local scenario experiments and solver-evaluated constrained placement. Existing flood tools already exist. No field accuracy or trained-model advantage is established.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One prepared site; finite soil storage; simplified diffusive-wave routing; editable rainfall; 2D fallback; local scenario export; every planner method named. No operational flood warnings or damage predictions. The master prompt governs full scope.

## Brand Commitments
RunoffLab; bone, ink, terra; clear grotesk typography; mono measurement labels; terrain dominates the instrument. Built by Rishik Rontala, with AI support disclosed.

## Evidence on Hand
CONCEPT-SHORTLIST.md, RESOURCE-PLAN.md, RUNOFFLAB-MASTER-PROMPT.md. No code or acquired terrain existed at session start. Winner descriptions were researched previously; their videos have not been watched.

## Product Principles
- Computed quantities drive the display.
- Every water transfer has a destination.
- Reproduce the same storm before comparing designs.
- Distinguish observed data, assumptions, and AI output.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard candidate selection, labeled units, text map summary, readable water legend, reduced motion, mobile 2D view, cancelable worker computation.
